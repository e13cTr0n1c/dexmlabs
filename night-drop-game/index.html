Night Drop

A quiet night over Lincolnshire. Fly the little post plane low, air drop parcels onto the chimneys, read the wind and keep the combo going before the clock runs out.

Ready for take off

Drag up or down to change height, tap to drop. On a keyboard use the arrows and Space. Line the marker up with a chimney.

How to play

The plane flies on its own. You choose the height and the moment to let go. The dotted line shows roughly where a parcel will land with the wind as it is now, so drop when the marker sits over a glowing chimney. Lower drops are easier to place, higher drops give the wind more time to push. Three hits in a row raise your multiplier, a miss resets it. Perfect hits earn the most.

In December and up to Twelfth Night the run turns into Christmas Eve, with snow, presents and golden stars that add three seconds to the clock if you fly through them.

About

I'm Arthur. I like the idea of a tiny plane doing the rounds over the flat fields at night, so I made one. Everything is drawn in the browser and your best score stays on this device. If something looks wrong, email me.
