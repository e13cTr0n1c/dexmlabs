How points work

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Points are shared by every game on the site. You earn them by playing and spend them on hints. They live in this browser, so there's no account and nothing to buy.

Your points show here when JavaScript is on.

Earning

WhatPoints
Finish today's Skywave round with at least one contact100
Decode today's Lorenz100
Read today's Lorenz in hard mode250
Send the hard mode reply back100
Finish a practice round in either game20, up to 3 a day in each game
Your first visit150 to start with

Each daily round pays once. If you'd already finished today's round before points arrived, you get them the next time you open that game.

Spending

HintPoints
Skywave: MUF or D loss, for one station10
Skywave: band scope, for one station20
Lorenz: check a wheel20
Lorenz: read the smudge50
Lorenz hard mode: reveal the QEP40
Lorenz hard mode: reveal a character10

Each hint button shows its price. If you don't have enough, the button says so and stays off until you've earned more. Hints don't take anything off your game score any more, so the score is just how you played. Wrong answers still cost score, as before.

Keeping it fair

Your points are a short list of what you earned and spent, with a check on each line. If the list gets edited, I keep the lines that still check out and drop the rest. If nothing checks out, you start again with 150. Only the last 200 lines are kept, and older ones are added up into a starting total.

Clearing them

Points are stored under dexm:points in your browser's localStorage. Clearing the site data for dexmlabs.app resets them. See cookies and storage.
