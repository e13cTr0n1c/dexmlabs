      How to play

      Open any flight tracker, or look up when you hear one. Type the callsign and press Catch it. If you are a guest, this browser checks the feed and keeps the card here. If you are signed in, the server checks the feed and keeps the card on your account. You do not choose the rarity. The aircraft type does.

      Rarity comes from the aircraft type. Common cards are worth 10 points, uncommon 25, rare 60, legendary 150 and mythic 400. A type you already own adds XP instead. Each callsign on the same aircraft counts once every six hours once you are signed in. As a guest, each callsign counts once a day, the same way as before.

      Questions
      How do I catch a plane?
      Find a plane on a flight tracker or spot one overhead, then type its callsign. If it is flying right now, its card goes in your hangar. As a guest you can still type a registration or a hex code.
      Why didn't it count?
      It has to be airborne at that moment. A plane on the ground, one that has gone quiet, or one the receivers cannot hear does not count.
      Where is my collection kept?
      As a guest, in this browser only. Export saves a file and Import brings it back. When you sign in, a saved hangar can come across once. After that, new catches are stored for your account and only the server can add them.
      What is the Legacy badge?
      Cards brought across from an older save wear it, so you can tell them from catches checked live.
      What if I have no account set up?
      The page still plays. Google, GitHub and the email link run a practice account in this browser and follow the same rules.

      About

      I love watching the big jets go over, so I made a way to collect them. The live data comes from adsb.lol, a community network that shares it openly. Thank you to everyone who feeds it.
