Tools

Estimates, not advice.

Free calculators for UK money questions: CIS tax for construction trades, and Inheritance Tax and probate in England and Wales. They run in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Each one links to the GOV.UK rules it uses. They give estimates, not tax, legal or financial advice. Check anything that matters with HMRC or a qualified adviser.

TOOL 01 / LIVECIS

CIS tax refund estimator

For subcontractors. Estimate your CIS refund or tax bill for 2025/26 or 2026/27, and see which Self Assessment boxes the figures go in.

Open tool →
TOOL 02 / LIVECIS

CIS deduction calculator

For contractors and subcontractors. Check what should come off an invoice at 20%, 30% or gross: labour only, with materials and VAT left out.

Open tool →
TOOL 03 / LIVEPROBATE

Probate and Inheritance Tax calculator

For England and Wales. Estimate the Inheritance Tax on an estate, with the residence allowance, gifts and the probate fee.
