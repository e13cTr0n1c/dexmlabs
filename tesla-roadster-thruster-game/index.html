A sports car with thrusters


In 2017 Tesla showed a new Roadster and said it would reach 60 mph in 1.9 seconds. In 2018 Elon Musk wrote that a “SpaceX option package” would add about ten small thrusters around the car. In mid October 2026, at a SpaceX test site in Texas, Tesla showed a prototype jumping, rolling in the air and landing on cold gas thrusters, with suction fans holding it to an upside down ramp, according to reports by AP, The Washington Post, Space.com and Aviation Week. Reported figures include about 0.81 seconds from 0 to 60 mph, ten thrusters and roughly 250 kg of compressed air. Musk said production should start in 2028.


This game is my take on that idea. It is not a model of the real car. The car is a generic red wedge, about 4 m long, and every number in it is mine.


Thruster Sprint


Tyres can only push as hard as their grip allows, about 1.4 g in the game, and the motor's power runs out as you speed up. The rear thruster does not care about grip: it pushes the whole car along its own axis, so with it lit you pull more than twice as hard and 60 mph comes up in well under a second. Hold D to drive and Space to boost. A gap in the strip needs the lift thrusters: raise them with W a moment before the edge, keep the nose level with Q and E, and cut them with X once you are over. Land with the car flat and the springs soak it up; land nose first and they don't.


Hover Hops


The lift thrusters fire straight out of the bottom of the car, so lean it and part of the push goes sideways, just like the booster in my Booster Landing Game. W and S set the lift and it stays where you leave it. About 77% holds the car still. Climb above the next platform before you drift across, or you will hit its side. Then level off, come down slowly and stop on the green pad. Over 9 m/s at touchdown breaks it, and a landing within a few degrees of flat scores best.


Space Drive


There is no road and no air, so nothing slows you down. Every push you make you have to cancel later with another push the other way, and that costs gas twice. The thrusters are fixed to the car: W and S push up and down, A and D back and forward, along the car's own axes, so turn the car with Q and E and its thrusters turn with it. Near Mars, gravity bends your path. Collect every star in any order, then stop on the beacon at under 1.5 m/s. A mannequin in a white suit rides along, a nod to the Starman mannequin that has been riding a Roadster round the Sun since 2018.


Scoring


Every finished run scores 200 plus parts for how well you did. Sprints score for the time, the 0 to 60 time, the speed at the line, flat landings over gaps and gas left. Hops score for a soft landing, a flat landing, stopping near the centre of the pad, time and gas. Space drives score for the stars, a gentle stop on the beacon, the centre, time and gas. Harder levels multiply the score, and you get a grade from S to D. Your best on each level is kept on this device.

Questions


What is the Tesla Roadster thruster package?
In June 2018 Elon Musk wrote that a “SpaceX option package” for the next generation Tesla Roadster would include about ten small rocket thrusters arranged around the car, to improve acceleration, top speed, braking and cornering. At an unveiling in mid October 2026 at a SpaceX test site in McGregor, Texas, Tesla showed a Roadster prototype using cold gas thrusters to jump, roll in the air and land, as reported by AP, The Washington Post, Space.com and Aviation Week.
What are cold gas thrusters?
A cold gas thruster lets compressed gas out through a nozzle. Nothing burns, so it is simple and safe compared with a rocket engine, but it gives far less push for each kilogram of gas. Spacecraft use them for small moves. Tesla reportedly stores compressed air in carbon fibre overwrapped pressure vessels; Space.com reported about 600 litres, or roughly 250 kg of air, feeding ten thrusters.
How fast is the new Roadster from 0 to 60 mph?
At the 2017 unveiling Tesla said 1.9 seconds for the base car. For the thruster car shown in October 2026, Aviation Week reported Tesla saying about 0.81 seconds, and The Washington Post reported under a second. These are the company's figures as reported, not independent tests. The car in this game is tuned to feel close to that, but its numbers are my own.
Can you buy the flying Roadster?
Not yet. Reports of the October 2026 event describe three versions: a road ready Performance car, a Thrust car with rear acceleration thrusters, and a single seat Rocket version that is invite only and, Tesla says, not approved for public roads. Musk said production should begin in 2028. Prices, dates and details may change, so check Tesla's own site.
Is there really a Tesla Roadster in space?
Yes, the original Roadster, not the new one. On 6 February 2018 the first Falcon Heavy carried Elon Musk's own first generation Roadster into space with a mannequin in a spacesuit, nicknamed Starman, in the driver's seat. It went into an orbit around the Sun that crosses the orbit of Mars. The Space Drive mode is a nod to that, with a generic mannequin.
Is the physics real?
The rules are real: Newton's laws, gravity, a power and grip limited drive, air drag, sprung wheels that compress when you land, thrust that points where the car points, and point gravity near Mars in the space levels. The sizes, forces and gas numbers are invented to make it fun. They are not Tesla data.
